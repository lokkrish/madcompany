# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Screens.** `mc_screen` records each screen of the app: its route (`/tasks/:id`, `/tasks/[id]` and hash routes match), the stories and UX requirements it implements, and its mockups. UI tickets name the screen they build in `refs` (`SCR-n`), which traces them to its stories. `mc_screens` lists them; HQ → Preview shows them and lets you add one. A screen's card shows its stories, the tickets that built it, its mockups and its comments; a requirement's card shows the screens implementing it.
- **Comments land on their screen.** A comment from the app's Comment button, a mockup or HQ is matched to its screen by route or mockup, so the lead sees which stories it touches before classifying it.
- **Three kinds of change.** `mc_map_change` takes `level` for comments: `element` (how it looks on one screen; amends nothing, the screen is enough; its impact is that screen, the tickets that built it and its mockups), `ux` (how it works; amends or extends a UX requirement or story, and its impact reaches every screen implementing it, their tickets and mockups) or `story` (what it does). A comment can't be started until it's classified. Comments that amend count for stale-knowledge diagnostics like change requests; element changes aren't listed for consistency checks.
- **Batch or send now.** Every comment, and a change request from the Inbox, is either sent now (the lead handles it at once) or added to your open **review** (`REV-n`). Batched comments wait quietly. When you submit the review, the lead runs `/mc-review-feedback`: `mc_feedback_review` shows each comment with its screen, level and sketches, what they touch together, and the checks the plan must answer (comments amending the same item or on the same element, other changes to what they amend or build on, open conflicts, decisions and the human's answers on anything they amend or build on or on their screens, built tickets implementing what they amend, stories still worded the old way, agreed contracts). `mc_feedback_plan` proposes one plan: an item per comment (fix, split, merge, decline), new wording, new requirements, tickets, reopenings, superseded decisions and an answer to every check. HQ refuses a plan with gaps. You approve it once in HQ → Reviews (or send it back with notes); HQ applies all of it and records your OK as a decision that settles what the plan answered. A plan that no longer holds when you approve it goes back to the lead. A comment can be taken out of a review (**Handle now**) or the review discarded.
- **Sketches.** Capture the screen (the browser asks to share the tab), or start from a mockup, a pasted image or a blank page, then draw, add arrows, boxes and text. The sketch is saved with the comment (`.madcompany/shots`), the lead sees it when planning, and the agent who claims the comment is told to open it first. In the app's Comment button, in HQ → Preview, on mockups, and on any comment ticket (**Add a sketch**).
- **Comments on mockups.** Click anywhere on a mockup image in HQ to pin a comment there (pins show on the image), or pick an element on an HTML mockup (it stays sandboxed: the picker only tells HQ which element you clicked, and HQ shows the form).
- **Comments from elsewhere.** `mc_comment` lets the lead or PM file a comment the human made in chat, on a Claude artifact or in a call, with its source, so it's traced and batched like the rest.
- New: `ui/sketch.js` and `ui/pick.js`; the app's Comment button has a form (kind of change, Sketch, Add to review / Send now) and shows your open review. HQ: a **Reviews** page, the plan waiting for your OK on the dashboard and in the Inbox, and screen, kind, review and sketches on ticket pages.
- File formats: new events `screen.upsert`, `ticket.batch`, `review.open`, `review.submit`, `review.plan`, `review.decide`, `review.applied`, `review.cancel`; `ticket.create` may carry `screen`, `where`, `level`, `batch` and `from`; `ticket.map` may carry `level`; `ticket.attach` has kind `sketch`. New readable files `.madcompany/log/screens.md` and `.madcompany/log/reviews.md`.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-03)

- **Project index.** HQ maps every requirement, story, epic, release, ticket, change request, decision, question, fact, note, design doc, meeting and message, and the typed links between them (implements, part of, depends on, amends, extends, discussed in…), from its event log and the anchored planning docs. No model reads files to build it.
- New agent tools: `mc_ref` (everything linked to an ID), `mc_section` (just an ID's definition), `mc_note` (what a member learned about a story or requirement), and for the lead or PM `mc_map_change`, `mc_requirement` and `mc_trace`. `mc_decide`, `mc_ask`, `mc_escalate` and `mc_minutes` take `refs`; `mc_decide` takes `supersedes`; `mc_create_ticket` and `mc_update_ticket` take `kind` (`task` or `chore`).
- `mc_claim` returns `context`: the ticket's requirements as they stand now (amended wording first) with their decisions, open questions, facts, notes and other tickets. `facts` leaves out facts known to concern only other requirements (`facts_elsewhere` says how many).
- **Traceability**, `traceability: strict | warn | off` in `team.yaml`. Strict (new projects): tickets need refs that trace to a requirement or are chores; decisions, questions and escalations name their ticket or requirements; agents' messages outside a ticket thread name a ticket or requirement; unknown IDs are refused. Projects without the setting get `warn`.
- **Change requests map to the requirements they amend or extend**, when raised (Inbox fields, `/api/change`) or with `mc_map_change`. An unmapped change or feedback ticket can't be started in strict mode. Mapping returns and posts the impact, and in spec mode puts the change in the epic it changes, so it merges with that epic instead of on a branch of its own. Amended wording and new requirements go to `.madcompany/log/requirements.md`.
- **Amendments are proposed until their change is done.** Agents see the current wording and the proposal side by side (the one building the change is told so). A change split into tickets is closed by HQ when they're all merged. A change nobody started can be withdrawn (`mc_withdraw_change`, HQ ticket page, `/api/withdraw`): its untouched tickets go with it, its proposed wording drops, and requirements it added no longer count.
- **Old knowledge is flagged after a change:** stories covering an amended requirement, decisions made on it and the human's answers that trace to it are diagnostics until reworded, superseded or confirmed; cards and claim context mark them `before_change`. Superseded decisions leave agents' context. Claim context now includes the human's answers given to questions about a requirement.
- **Contradictions without a shared ID.** The index finds requirements, stories, decisions and answers close in wording to an item but not linked to it ("possibly related", in `mc_ref`, claim context and change mapping). `mc_conflict` raises a contradiction to whoever settles it (use-case → product manager, tech → architect, else the lead). `mc_change` lets the lead or PM raise a change request, which waits for an owner's OK in HQ → Inbox. `mc_check` and `/mc-check` record consistency reviews of new stories, requirements and changes; unchecked ones are listed. PM and architect roles settle conflicts.
- Diagnostics for the lead's briefing, `mc_trace`, HQ → Trace and `npx madcompany trace [--strict]`: unmapped changes, work done (or in progress) before a change amended its requirement, unknown IDs, reopened dependencies, open questions on reviewed tickets, untraced work.
- HQ: a **Trace** page (coverage, what to fix, every requirement with its tickets and changes, add a requirement), a page per ID (`#/ref/<ID>`), "Traces to" and change mapping on ticket pages, Amends/Extends on the change request form, and a traceability line on the dashboard.
- CLI: `ref <ID>` and `trace [--strict]`.
- Agent protocol: look IDs up with `mc_ref`/`mc_section`, raise contradictions with the lead, leave `mc_note`s. Reviewing is now step 10. `/mc-plan-release` defines requirements when the planning docs have none; `/mc-scan` records an existing app's capabilities as requirements; `/mc-ui-sprint` maps feedback; `/mc-minutes` passes `refs`.
- **Fewer refused commands.** In real runs, agents lost turns to commands Claude Code refuses whatever the allow list says (brackets around commands, `cd` or `git -C` into another folder, `cat > file`), then filed Human help to "allow" `git` and `npm test`, which already were. The agent protocol and `/mc-start` now spell out the shapes that run and to use Write, Edit, Glob and Grep instead. `init` also allows `git ls-files`, `git ls-tree`, `git show-ref`, `git rev-list`, `head`, `tail`, `grep`, `wc` and `pwd` (run `npx madcompany init` again to add them to an existing project).
- Resuming a parked (blocked) ticket no longer counts as an attempt: waiting on an answer, an approval or Human help isn't a failed try. Fresh starts and restarts after review or a reopen still count.
- File formats: new events `consistency.check`, `ticket.approve`, `ticket.withdraw`, `ticket.map`, `requirement.add`, `requirement.amend`, `note.add`; `ticket.create` may carry `amends`/`extends`; `question.ask`, `decision.log` and `minutes.add` may carry `refs` (`question.ask` also `conflict`; a team-raised `ticket.create` `approval` and `raisedBy`) (and `decision.log` `supersedes`). New readable files `.madcompany/log/requirements.md` and `.madcompany/log/notes.md`.

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-25)

First version.

- HQ: dashboard, chat, Jira-style board, ticket pages, inbox, decisions, team, preview and a file viewer with clickable IDs, all updating live
- An MCP server with 34 tools for agents and the lead: tickets, park/resume checkpoints, reviews, questions, escalations, decisions, design docs, memory, per-agent environments, and `mc_wait` for the lead
- Rules enforced in code: dependencies, review by someone other than the author, screenshots on UI tickets, attempt limits, memory size limits, question ping-pong detection, owner-only design docs
- Safety hook: Stop now, plus a deny list for push, deploy, cloud changes, publishing, global installs and `.env` secrets
- Merge queue: epic branches, checks re-run after each merge, revert and reopen on failure
- BMad bridge: anchors requirement, epic and story IDs, links bare references, imports stories as tickets with dependencies
- Claude Code integration: `/mc-*` skills and one subagent per team member, each in its own worktree
- Library in HQ: planning files, UX spec and HTML mockups, architecture and design docs, epics, minutes of meetings, Claude Code conversations and links (Claude artifacts first), sorted by stage, plus search across everything
- Claude artifacts are captured automatically: a PostToolUse hook saves each published artifact to HQ (queued while HQ is off), and past ones are found in session transcripts
- `/mc-minutes` and the `mc_minutes` / `mc_link` tools
- Per-agent model picker in HQ's Team page (writes `team.yaml` and regenerates the agent)
- Role catalogue: 14 roles in 7 departments (`type:` in `team.yaml`), each with a title, domain, default model, permissions and duties
- Team templates: `staff --template small|medium|large` for a 5, 10 or 20-person company
- Hire and remove agents from HQ's Team page, grouped by department
- Multi-user HQ: `people:` in `team.yaml` with owner, member and viewer roles; `people add|link|remove|list`; `hq --share` with personal sign-in links (stored hashed, HttpOnly cookie); agents and the CLI stay local-only
- Five development modes (`mode:` in `team.yaml`, `init --mode`, `madcompany mode`): UI-driven, MVP-driven, UI-MVP (default), brownfield and spec-driven
- Releases (R1, R2…) for the release-based modes: `/mc-plan-release`, `release/rN` branches, review in HQ → Releases (approve or request changes), release notes, and `madcompany ship` to merge an approved release into main and tag it
- Human help: everything only a person can do (accounts, keys, service setup, payments, push and deploy, access) in one HQ page with steps, `.env` key checks that never read values, and parked tickets that resume when an item is done; agents file items with `mc_human_help`
- Brownfield onboarding: `madcompany scan` and `/mc-scan` write a codebase map and set the real checks
- MCP servers and plugins: HQ → Tools lists servers, plugins, skills and plugin agents; each agent learns its own servers by role; `mc_tools`; the safety hook lets read-only MCP tools run unattended and sends pushes, deploys, payments, deletes and remote writes to Human help; `tools.allow` / `tools.human` in `team.yaml`; server suggestions for your stack; a call log without inputs
- GitHub Pages site (`site/`, `npm run site`) with the overview, the five workflows and a command reference
- `madcompany demo` for a sample project
- Fix: Library search and the reference scanner no longer read through symlinks that lead outside the project, and a broken link no longer crashes HQ's views (found by the first macOS CI run)
- File formats: `.madcompany/team.yaml`, `log/events.jsonl` (append-only; readable copies in `log/*.md`), `ids.json`, `agents/<id>/{identity,work,comms}.md`
